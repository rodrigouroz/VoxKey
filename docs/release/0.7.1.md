# VoxKey 0.7.1

## Live-preview improvements

- Reduce repeated preview decoding during longer phrases while preserving the
  initial preview timing and the full preview context.
- Catch previews up after a pause, including short utterances and pauses with
  quiet background sound. Catch-ups survive busy decoding and rapidly resumed
  speech, use the newest audio, and remain rate-limited.
- Keep preview failures isolated from capture and final recognition. Preview
  words remain presentation-only; only final transcription reaches the editor.

## Reliability fixes

- Find focused editors in deeply nested windows without rejecting them because
  of unrelated nested content. Node and time limits, cycle detection, ambiguous
  focus rejection, and secure-field protection remain in place.
- Fix audio-resampler compilation with newer Swift toolchains while retaining
  the shared, synchronized converter callback state.

## Privacy

Production builds contain no VoxKey telemetry, performance recording, debug
logging, transcription tracing, or diagnostic controls. These facilities require
explicit local-only compile flags and are excluded from the shipped executable.
Saved preferences cannot enable them in production.

## Validation limits

Automated coverage includes preview scheduling, pause/resume behavior, busy
decoding, final-audio preservation, focused-editor resolution, and delivery safety.
Model checks use synthetic speech; they do not establish battery impact, accuracy,
or latency across all Macs and editors. The maintainer reports successful recent
daily use, but a fresh-user installation, physical microphone compatibility
matrix, VoiceOver walkthrough, and installed Sparkle update have not been repeated
for this release. The previously reported intermittent Right Command activation
failure remains unresolved.
