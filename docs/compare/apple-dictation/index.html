VoxKey vs
Apple Dictation

Compare built-in macOS Dictation with VoxKey’s local-model workflow.

Researched September 14, 2026 · VoxKey 0.6.0

When to choose VoxKey

Choose VoxKey if you want to pick your Whisper model, add vocabulary hints, hold a key to dictate, and recover a result when delivery cannot be confirmed.

When to choose Apple Dictation

Try Apple Dictation first if you want speech to text without installing another app. It comes with macOS and supports spoken punctuation, formatting commands, and many languages.

The practical differences
