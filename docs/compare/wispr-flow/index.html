LOCAL VS CLOUD

VoxKey vs
Wispr Flow

Compare local dictation with a cloud service across desktop and mobile.

Researched September 14, 2026 · VoxKey 0.6.0

When to choose VoxKey

Choose VoxKey if you want free dictation that works without a connection after model setup, and you use an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 26 or later.

When to choose Wispr Flow

Choose Wispr Flow if you want dictation across Mac, Windows, iOS, and Android, plus shared dictionaries and team administration. An internet connection is required.

The practical differences
