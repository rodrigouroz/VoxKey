When to choose VoxKey

Choose VoxKey when the job is speaking into your current text field, then getting back to writing. It offers local speech-model selection, vocabulary hints, and recovery without a saved dictation archive.

When to choose MacWhisper

Choose MacWhisper if you also transcribe audio or video files, export subtitles, or need batch processing. It offers local transcription as well as optional AI integrations.

Before you switch

VoxKey is for dictation into text fields. It has no meeting recorder, file-transcription workspace, or full voice-control system. Optional text improvement works only with English selected, defaults off, and includes a noncommercial-only grammar model. Editor compatibility and real-world latency are still being tested.
